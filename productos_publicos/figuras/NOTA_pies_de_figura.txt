Maps are limited to the study-area box (UTM 17N, E 360.0-365.0 km, N 2555.9-2561.9 km). No administrative boundaries are drawn. The boundaries shown are for reference only (coastline derived from the water mask of the Sentinel-2 scenes).
a: median turbidity per pixel (Dogliotti et al. 2015 algorithm, ACOLITE DSF), 85 accepted scenes at 10 m; colour scale clipped to the 2nd-98th percentile; FNU values are not calibrated against in situ data.
b: median across scenes of the per-scene median in 250 m bins along the arm axis (+-150 m corridor); band = 95 % bootstrap over scenes.
c: groups of pixels with climatological z_c >= 3.5; colour = number of independent halves (2016-2020, 2021-2025) with a hot pixel within 80 m; depth = median of the 1887 chart (no dredging) within 80 m; chart error sigma 0.9 m, datum uncertainty 0 to +0.5 m. The chart depth comes from the SCHISM mesh of the repository Modelo-hidrodin-mico (commit 72095a8, docs/procedencia_datos.md, where its source conditions are listed); the depth grid itself is not redistributed here.
