Maps are limited to the study-area box (UTM 17N, E 360.0-365.0 km, N 2555.9-2561.9 km). No administrative boundaries are drawn. The boundaries shown are for reference only (coastline derived from the water mask of the Sentinel-2 scenes).
a: median turbidity per pixel (Dogliotti et al. 2015 algorithm, ACOLITE DSF), 85 accepted scenes at 10 m; colour scale clipped to the 2nd-98th percentile; FNU values are not calibrated against in situ data.
b: median across scenes of the per-scene median in 250 m bins along the arm axis (+-150 m corridor); band = 95 % bootstrap over scenes.
c: groups of pixels with climatological z_c >= 3.5; colour = number of independent halves (2016-2020, 2021-2025) with a hot pixel within 80 m; depth = median of the 1887 chart (no dredging) within 80 m; chart error sigma 0.9 m, datum uncertainty 0 to +0.5 m. The chart depths are not included in the CSV or redistributed here; to reproduce panel c, obtain the chart and read the soundings: U.S. Hydrographic Office, chart no. 307, "West Indies, Cuba, harbor of Havana: from the most recent Spanish surveys to 1887", ed. 1898, Boston Public Library, Leventhal Map & Education Center (call no. G9096.P5 svar .U55 no. 307 1898; https://collections.leventhalmap.org/search/commonwealth:g445ht01p, as recorded on 2 Oct 2026 in the model repository; not re-verified on 19 Nov 2026). Sounding positions were georeferenced on the 1889 edition of the Harbor of Havana chart (Harvard Geospatial Library, https://hgl.harvard.edu/catalog/harvard-g4924-h3-1889-u5; check its terms of use). The procedure and its parameters are in the repository Modelo-hidrodin-mico (commit 72095a8, docs/procedencia_datos.md).
